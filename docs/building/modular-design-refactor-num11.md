## 2. Scoping ("build new #11")

§11 has 14 items, ranging from a one-line tweak to multi-week rewrites.

### Small, mechanical (hours)
1. `dependency-cruiser` for intra-package cycles
2. Per-package coverage gate (≥90% lines / ≥80% branches)
3. `tsconfig` project references
4. `/resume /permissions /image` in `parseChatCommand`: only if a core-side consumer exists (none does today)

### Medium, real design seams (about a day each)
5. `ProbeLoop` contract in `models`, `createProbeLoop` in `agent-loop`, with `setup` taking it injected. Removes `setup→agent-loop`.
6. `Interaction` FIFO queue + typed `Prompt<A>` + `pending()`. The continue question, plan gate and cost card become typed prompts.
7. `Permissions.decide/ask` facade. The gate moves from `runs` into `permissions`, which then depends on `interaction`.
8. `ChannelService` facade with `health()`
9. `RunStrategy.plan(req) → TaskGraph` shape
10. `ToolRegistry.for(role)` method form

### Large, sweeping (weeks; touch every module)
11. `Result<T, StewartError>` on every fallible cross-module call
12. §10 five-phase setup session (`SetupService`, `SetupSession`, drafts, undo, new protocol methods)
13. `RunHandle.events` AsyncIterable. Conflicts with the event-push protocol; recommendation: don't build.
14. `v1import → config`. Blocked by layering (needs `catalog`); recommendation: leave it.

Decisions:
- Build Small 1–3 first.
- Then the Medium items in dependency order: 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 → 10.
- The coverage gate is met by writing tests to 90/80, not by a ratchet baseline.
- Cycles are fixed with interfaces, keeping `tsPreCompilationDeps: true`.
